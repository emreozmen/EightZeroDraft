## Full Description

Build your dream World Cup draft squad and chase the perfect 8-0 tournament run.

In 8-0 World Cup Draft, every run starts with a coin toss. Lock your formation, draw your manager, then draft players from historic World Cup squads. Pick carefully: every position matters, every rating shapes your team, and every match can end the run.

Create your XI, simulate the tournament, survive the group stage, push through the knockouts, and try to lift the cup.

Features:

- Coin toss draft system
- Historic World Cup squad pool
- Formation and manager drafts
- Manual position-based lineup building
- DEF, MID and ATT team ratings
- Live match simulation with goals, cards and key moments
- Group stage, knockouts, extra time and penalties
- Tournament result card to share
- Light and dark mode

Can you build the perfect squad and go 8-0?
