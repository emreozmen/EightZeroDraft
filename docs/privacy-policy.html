Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how 8-0 World Cup Draft Game handles information. The app is a football draft and tournament simulation game.

Information We Collect

We do not require an account, login, email address, phone number, contact list, location, camera, microphone, or payment information to play the game.

The app does not intentionally collect personal information from players.

Gameplay Data

The app may store gameplay progress, settings, draft state, language preference, theme preference, and generated result cards locally on your device. This information is used only to run the game experience and is not sent to our servers.

Sharing

The app may let you create or share a tournament result image. Sharing happens only when you choose to use your device's sharing features or third-party apps such as social or messaging apps. Those third-party services are governed by their own privacy policies.

Analytics, Ads, and Tracking

The current version of the app does not include third-party advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking.

Children's Privacy

The app is a general audience sports game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-Party Platforms

If you download the app from the App Store or Google Play, Apple or Google may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies. We do not control those platform-level practices.

Data Deletion

Because the app does not require an account and does not intentionally collect personal information on our servers, there is no account data to delete. You can delete local gameplay data by removing the app from your device.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app changes. If we add analytics, ads, accounts, online features, or new data collection, this policy and the store privacy disclosures should be updated before release.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us through the support contact listed on the app's store page.
